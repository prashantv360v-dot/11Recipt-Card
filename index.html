Creamy Garlic Pasta

A delicious, creamy pasta made with garlic, fresh herbs, and Parmesan cheese. Perfect for a quick homemade meal.

⏱️
Prep Time 10 min
🍳
Cook Time 20 min
🍽️
Servings 2 people

Ingredients

Everything you need

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup fresh cream
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • Fresh parsley, chopped

Preparation Steps

Follow these simple steps

  1. Cook the pasta

    Boil water in a large pot, add salt and pasta, and cook according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.

  2. Sauté the garlic

    Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.

  3. Make the creamy sauce

    Pour in the cream, add Parmesan cheese, black pepper, and salt. Stir until the sauce becomes smooth.

  4. Combine everything

    Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and mix well. Cook for another 2 minutes so the pasta absorbs the flavors.

  5. Serve and enjoy

    Garnish with fresh parsley and extra Parmesan cheese. Serve immediately while warm.
